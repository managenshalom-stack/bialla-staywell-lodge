Our Story

Bialla Staywell Lodge was established to give families, community groups, and visiting workers a comfortable and affordable place to stay in West New Britain. What started as a small guesthouse has grown into a trusted lodge known for its warm hospitality and convenient location.

We understand that whether you're travelling for a family event, a work assignment, or a community gathering, you need a place that feels welcoming from the moment you arrive.

Our Mission

To provide clean, comfortable, and affordable accommodation for the local community and visitors to West New Britain, while supporting community events and gatherings with reliable venue space.

Meet the Team

Our small team of local staff takes pride in looking after every guest personally. From check-in to check-out, we're here to make your stay easy and comfortable.

Quick Facts

  • Located in Bialla, West New Britain Province
  • Family-owned and locally operated
  • Rooms available for short and extended stays
  • Function space available for community events
